Privacy Policy

This policy explains how EyeCue handles information when you use the public EyeCue Android app.

Last updated: November 10, 2026

Camera and detection

EyeCue asks for camera permission to detect objects and provide spoken or visual alerts. The public EyeCue app processes camera frames on your device. It does not save or upload camera images or video. You can revoke camera permission in your device settings; detection will not work without it.

Information on your device

EyeCue stores app preferences on your device, including settings you choose. If you import a detector model, the app stores an app-owned copy and information needed to use it. Temporary detection and performance information may be held in memory while the app runs.

The public EyeCue release does not include the research-session recording and upload feature. This policy applies to EyeCue, not the separate EyeCue Study app.

Internet and other services

The public EyeCue release does not request Android internet permission and does not upload research sessions to the EyeCue server. EyeCue uses your device's selected text-to-speech engine for spoken alerts. That engine is provided separately by your device or another provider; depending on its settings, it may use network services or download voice data under that provider's privacy policy. Android and other system services operate under their providers' policies.

Retention and deletion

Settings and imported models remain in app storage until replaced or removed, app storage is cleared, or the app is uninstalled. Clearing app storage or uninstalling deletes the app's local data from the device. If your device or Google account has made a backup, manage that backup separately using your device or account settings.

Children

EyeCue does not require an account or ask users to provide their names or email addresses within the app. Parents or guardians can control camera access and remove the app through device settings.

Changes to this policy

We may update this page when EyeCue or its data practices change. The date above shows when this policy was last updated.
